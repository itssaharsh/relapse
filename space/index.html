Final results · registered before they ran · official tokenizer

An outdated tokenizer setting made unlearning look deeper than it was: at the default layers, not at layers 3 to 5.

We made Cohere's small Tiny Aya models forget hazardous biology with Lexsi Labs' SafeTune, then ran them the way people do: shrunk to 4-bit files, and through the official tokenizer, the step that cuts text into the pieces a model reads. The forgetting mostly held in 4-bit files, but when a model was unlearned and checked through an older tokenizer setting at SafeTune's default layers, the check overstated the forgetting: about half of it was gone again through the official tokenizer. At layers 3 to 5 that did not happen on four of the five models. The layers are not shown to be the cause: every run that came back had also lost general knowledge where it was checked.

Our first-round claims were withdrawn after we found that tokenizer problem in our own runs. What we got wrong

The hazard score is the share of questions answered correctly on WMDP-bio, a public multiple-choice test of hazardous biology knowledge. Lower is better: with four choices, 25% is guessing, and the original models score to . Q4_0 and NF4 are two common ways of storing a model in 4 bits per number instead of 16, which makes the file about a quarter of the size. MMLU is a broad general-knowledge test, and benign biology is its ordinary biology questions; for both, higher is better. Layers 3 to 5 cost more benign biology than the default layers do. Tiny Aya Base is not in the top rows: in the outdated setting it drops to guessing level at every placement and comes back up through the official tokenizer.

        What we got wrong

        Our first round of results (Stage 1) ran Tiny Aya through an outdated tokenizer setting. The copy of the model we used predated a fix Cohere made on Hugging Face, so text was split differently from the way the model was trained. The weights were fine; the scores were not. We withdrew the claims we had made on those models: that one of them relapsed in a 4-bit file, and that training on the file repaired it.

        Everything on this page is Stage 2. Each experiment was registered in the repository before it ran, and every model was scored through the official tokenizer. of the registered hypotheses failed, in full or in part, and every one is reported.
